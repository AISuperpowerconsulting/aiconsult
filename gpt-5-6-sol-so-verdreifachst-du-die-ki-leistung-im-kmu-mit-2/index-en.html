GPT-5.6 Sol: How to Triple AI Performance in SMEs with 2 Settings

Imagine tripling the efficiency of your AI applications overnight, without even training a new model. This is precisely what OpenAI achieved at the end of July 2026 with their GPT-5.6 Sol model on the demanding ARC-AGI-3 benchmark – an impressive increase from 13.3% to 38.3% by activating just two specific settings.

The Sensation: What OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol Teaches Us

The news struck the AI community like a lightning bolt: OpenAI, known for its groundbreaking models, has massively improved the performance of its GPT-5.6 Sol on the ARC-AGI-3 Benchmark. This benchmark, introduced in March 2026, is no easy feat; it tests the ability of AIs to independently derive goals and plan action sequences in interactive environments, entirely without explicit instructions. While most leading models initially scored below 1% and humans achieved 100%, GPT-5.6 Sol showed a leap from 13.3% to 38.3% – a threefold increase in score.

The fascinating aspect: This improvement did not come about through a renewed, costly retraining of the model. Rather, it was two 'Harness' software settings that made the difference. For Swiss SMEs, this is a crucial insight: It's not always about having the newest, largest, or most expensive AI. Often, the true potential lies in the intelligent use and configuration of existing models. This discovery shows that that even with currently available models like the GPT-5.6 family, which has been on the market since July 2026, enormous leaps in efficiency are possible if one understands and applies the 'rules of the game' of AI.

The First Silver Bullet: 'Retaining Reasoning' – Why Context is Invaluable for SMEs

The first of the two magical settings is called 'Retaining Reasoning' – that is, 'maintaining the argumentation' or 'retaining the line of reasoning'. Imagine you give an employee a complex task that requires multiple steps. If this employee were to forget the previous considerations and interim results with each new step, they would constantly have to start over or re-evaluate important information. That would be inefficient, frustrating, and prone to errors.

This is precisely what 'Retaining Reasoning' prevents in AI. It allows the model to store its complex thought processes and intermediate steps during a task and retrieve them when needed. For your SME, this specifically means: Your AI-powered customer service bot can handle a longer, branched customer inquiry much more coherently without losing the conversation context. Your AI assistant for marketing content creation can consistently develop a campaign over several weeks by drawing on previous briefings and iterations. Or your data analysis tool can track complex relationships across multiple query steps without you constantly having to redefine the starting point. The loss of context is a hidden cost factor that is significantly reduced by this setting, thereby substantially increasing the quality of AI results.

The Second Silver Bullet: 'Enabling Compaction' – Efficiency and Cost Control for Your Budget

The second crucial setting is 'Enabling Compaction'. It optimizes the internal representation and processing of information. Essentially, it works like this: Instead of storing the entire, often very long history of an interaction or thought process uncompressed, the AI summarizes old histories into a 'condensed package'. The model then carries this package into its new workspace without sacrificing important details.

What does this mean for your Swiss SME? Simply put: massive efficiency gains and cost savings. OpenAI reported that the number of output tokens – i.e., the 'text units' that the model generates and for which you pay – decreased by approximately sixfold. In today's world, where models like GPT-5.6 Sol and other models in the family (Terra, Luna) are becoming increasingly powerful, but the cost per token remains a factor, this is sensational news. Your budget for AI applications will be relieved, and at the same time, the AI can process more complex and longer tasks more efficiently. Think of the automatic generation of business reports, the summarization of long documents, or the processing of project documentation: With 'Enabling Compaction', this becomes not only faster but also significantly cheaper, as the AI has less to 'forget' and therefore requires fewer tokens for recapitulation.

Practical Relevance for Swiss SMEs: More Than Just API Settings

At first glance, it might seem as though these settings are only relevant for large tech giants. But nothing could be further from the truth! The principles behind 'Retaining Reasoning' and 'Enabling Compaction' are universal and crucial for any Swiss SME looking to successfully deploy AI. They demonstrate that the 'architecture' surrounding the actual AI – the so-called 'Harness' software or the 'Echoverse', as described by Microsoft Research in 2026 – is just as important as the model itself.

What can you learn from this? Firstly, when implementing AI solutions, pay attention to how well context is maintained over longer interactions. Does your chatbot lose track after three questions? If so, there's room for optimization. Secondly, question the efficiency of your AI workflows. Are unnecessarily many tokens being consumed because the AI constantly has to repeat or reprocess information? Smart orchestration can work wonders here.

Especially in the context of current LLM vulnerabilities and attack vectors in 2026, it is essential not only to look at the raw performance of the models but also at the robustness and efficiency of the surrounding systems. A well-designed 'Harness' architecture not only boosts performance but also enhances the security and reliability of your AI applications, which is indispensable for compliance and customer trust in Switzerland.
